The idea, Sir, of a man who never did any damage even to the most diminutive of German princes, being able to do harm to the gigantic British empire, would be, in our eyes, an insult to the English nation. We therefore beg leave to move that the whole matter be wound up by The Times giving a vote of thanks to Mr. Charles Heinzen, for the courage malheureux4 with which he combated Socialism and Communism. I am, Mr. Editor,

Yours very obediently,

A German Social Democrat

London, Nov. 28th. 1849
