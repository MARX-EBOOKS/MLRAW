Should the Opposition Government dissolve the House of Commons, the new elections will return, under the old conditions, the same result, viz., another House of Commons, in which the different parties will again paralyse themselves, when the old game has to be recommenced, and England's politics are plunged once more in a circle vicieux.

I, therefore, insist on the old dilemma: either Continuation of the Tory Government, or Parliamentary Reform.
