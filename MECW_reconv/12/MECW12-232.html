Revolution and Counter-Revolution, written for The Tribune some two years ago,a desire to have an immediate intuition of it, will do well to inspect the pictureb by Mr. Hasenclever, now being exhibited in the New-York Crystal Palace, representing the presentation of a workingmen's petition to the magistrates of Düsseldorf in 1848. What the writer could only analyze, the eminent painter has reproduced in its dramatic vitality.
