Now, my dear uncle, I bid you farewell. Despite carbuncles and furuncles, I consider the two months I have lived in your house, as one of the happiest episodes of my life, and I shall always feel thankful for the kindness you have shown me.

You will, of course, tell Rothhäuschen19 that I send him my compliments and that I regret having been forced giving battle to him.

My best compliments to the whole family, especially Jettchen20, Dr. Anrooy and Fritz21. Mrs. Marx and the girls send also their compliments. Please to give the enclosed lines to Netchen22.

Yours truly23
Charles M.
