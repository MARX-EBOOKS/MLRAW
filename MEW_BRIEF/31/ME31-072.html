My best compliments to Mrs. Burns. I am indeed very glad to hear that the o was an inorganic intrusion upon her name, and that she is a namesake of the great poet15. If Mrs. Gumpert declines becoming a member of a Workingmen’s Association, I hope Mrs. Burns will not follow that example, but will believe with her namesake that „a man is a man for all that“.16

Salut.

Dein
K. M.
