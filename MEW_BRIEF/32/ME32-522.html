5. Juli 1870

Dear Engels,

Thank you very much for your letter and the most interesting notes. I only hope Mr. Risse would interlard them with the Judenkirschen of which he seems to have a plentiful stock on hand. For O'D[onovan] Rossas portrait I have written to Pigott. In case a good photograph is not to be had, of course, I can as you say, send Kugelmann the print which appeared in the Irishman.

With many thanks, I remain

Affectionately yours

Jenny33
